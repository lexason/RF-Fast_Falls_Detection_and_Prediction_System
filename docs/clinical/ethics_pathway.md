# Ethics and research-governance pathway

Status: Draft roadmap; no HREC, exemption, institutional determination, or site approval is recorded.
Owner: Unassigned.

The NHMRC National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research 2025 has been in effect since
23 June 2026. The institution's research office must determine the required pathway for existing
self-testing, secondary use/publication of prior data, and every future protocol.

## Current safe boundary

Permitted without expanding this task: documentation, synthetic signals, offline replay, software
tests, and non-transmitting bench analysis that involves no person and meets local laboratory rules.

Not authorised by this roadmap:

- further staged falls or hazardous human manoeuvres;
- recording another person, resident, staff member, visitor, or bystander;
- clinical or aged-care deployment;
- live operational alerts or clinician-visible decision support;
- reuse, publication, or export of prior human-derived data without a documented determination.

## Required determinations and approvals

1. Identify institution, principal investigator/sponsor, HREC/research-governance contacts, and data
   custodian.
2. Obtain a written determination for existing researcher self-test data and its intended analyses,
   retention, publication, sharing, and withdrawal limits.
3. For healthy-adult controlled activities, approve recruitment, accessible information/consent,
   screening/exclusions, trained participants, supervision, mats, spotter, fatigue checks, stopping
   rules, incident response, compensation/insurance, and adverse-event reporting.
4. Do not induce falls in vulnerable participants. Older-adult work is limited to clinician-approved
   functional tasks and usual mobility aids.
5. For silent observation, approve site governance, no-alert operation, coverage zones, visible
   monitoring status, pause/opt-out, bystander/visitor handling, prospective incident adjudication,
   and privacy-preserving context.
6. For prospective clinical evaluation, lock the model and analysis, approve the human workflow,
   monitoring, fallback, safety oversight, and pre-specified outcomes.

## Consent and dignity

Consent must be ongoing and accessible, including capacity and communication needs. Participants
may decline monitoring, choose excluded zones, pause where policy permits, and understand what
historical data can or cannot be deleted after withdrawal. Monitoring cannot justify restrictive
practice. Bedroom, bathroom, nocturnal, continence, visitor, and cohabitant implications require
specific review.

## Ground truth and safety

Use the framework's ground-truth hierarchy and record label quality. Every human recording links to
the approved protocol/version, consent/legal-authority basis, supervisor, adverse-event record, and
stop checklist. No technical researcher may self-approve clinical safety.

## Gate evidence required

Approval identifiers, versions, dates, expiry/review dates, named owners, clinical partners, incident
and escalation workflow, data-management plan, consent materials, safety monitoring, and residual
risk approval must be linked before human data collection or G0/G3/G7 passage.
