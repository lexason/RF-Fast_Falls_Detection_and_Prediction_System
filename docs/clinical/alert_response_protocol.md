# Alert response protocol

Status: NOT APPROVED / NO OPERATIONAL CLINICAL ALERTS.

No current CARAT output may initiate care, emergency response, treatment, medication change, or
restrictive practice. The legacy dashboard's FALL ALERT wording is a development artifact and not
an approved clinical pathway.

A future protocol must name the clinical owner, site, authorised recipient, monitored population,
notification channels, response and acknowledgement bounds, acceptable alert budget, fallback,
downtime and no-signal handling, probable-event adjudication, injury assessment boundary,
documentation, escalation, disagreement/override, adverse-event reporting, and audit retention.

It must state:

- probable fall-like event is not proof of a fall or injury;
- absence of an alert is not proof of safety;
- unknown/abstention and low-quality data are visible and do not suppress ordinary care;
- model output supports review only;
- local emergency and post-fall protocols remain authoritative.

Clinical partner appointment and approval are required before this document can become operational.
