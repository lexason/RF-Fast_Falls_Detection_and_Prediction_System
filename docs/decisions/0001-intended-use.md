# ADR 0001: Investigational intended use and product boundaries

- Status: Proposed; clinical and governance approval pending
- Date: 2026-08-07
- Clinical owner: Unassigned
- Regulatory owner: Unassigned
- Privacy owner: Unassigned
- RF compliance owner: Unassigned

## Intended use

The system is an investigational, non-contact platform that estimates human activity, probable
fall events, and longitudinal fall-risk indicators for clinician review. It does not diagnose a
condition, prescribe treatment, replace clinical assessment, or autonomously determine care.

The intended user is an authorised clinician or researcher. The supported decision is whether
available evidence warrants review under an approved local workflow. No operational clinical
decision or alert pathway is approved at this stage.

## Separate products

1. Product A, fall event detection: seconds-scale probability, event timing, latency, uncertainty,
   and sensor quality.
2. Product B, activity and mobility recognition: seconds-to-minutes activity or transition state,
   including an explicit unknown output.
3. Product C, long-horizon fall-risk forecasting: calibrated absolute risk for a named future
   horizon using validated RF mobility and appropriate clinical inputs.
4. Product D, imminent fall warning: high-risk research only; it is not an available or approved
   capability and must not be advertised or deployed.

Labels, models, evaluation designs, metrics, and claims remain separate across these products.

## Safety and governance boundaries

- A clinician remains responsible for interpretation and action.
- A fall alert is not proof of injury; no alert is not proof of safety.
- Unknown or abstain is required when signal, identity association, coverage, data sufficiency, or
  model confidence is inadequate.
- No induced falls involving vulnerable participants are permitted.
- Human-participant collection requires an approved protocol, consent, HREC/site governance, and
  an escalation pathway. Self-testing claims in historical records do not establish those approvals.
- No treatment recommendation may be generated by a model.
- No clinical deployment or live operational alerting is authorised by this ADR.

## Preliminary regulatory position

Australian TGA guidance states that intended purpose determines whether software or AI is a
medical device, including software intended for monitoring or prediction of injury or disability.
This project therefore requires a formal, claim-specific TGA applicability determination before
clinical deployment. This ADR is not that determination.

The National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human Research 2025 is in effect from 23 June 2026.
The ethics pathway is HREC review plus institution and site governance before participant activity.

## Approval needed to close G0

Governance must approve the research purpose, intended users, decision and escalation workflow;
assign clinical, privacy, regulatory, and RF owners; identify clinical partners; and link the
formal ethics and TGA determinations. Until then this decision remains proposed and G0 fails.
